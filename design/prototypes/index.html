One Die Deep: three directions

Each prototype is built on the d6 and changes three things at once: the theme, the genre and one core mechanic. They are small and rough on purpose; the point is to feel the difference. Progress in NETDIVE and High Roller is saved in this browser. Decisions about each are in the design folder's README. Back to One Die Deep.

NETDIVE

Active: being explored further

Theme
Hacking a corporate network
Genre
Incremental: time-limited dives, permanent upgrades
Mechanic
One stat that moves

Your only stat is Bias. Attacks roll like Lasers & Feelings: pick Break or Spoof, roll its 1 to 3 dice, and count the hits. A die under your Bias hits for Break, over it for Spoof, and your Bias exactly is a zero-day that hits for either. Expertise upgrades add dice. Every hit moves your Bias, one way or the other depending on a setting you can switch.

Sim: choosing attacks well cracks the Core in a median of 18 dives under swing (10 under momentum); choosing at random takes 31 to 33.

SONAR

Abandoned: not pursued

Theme
A submarine in a deep-sea trench
Genre
Dungeon minesweeper
Mechanic
No stats, only a level

Ping cells of a dark 8×8 trench. Open water echoes the total power of the creatures around it. Pinging a creature fights it: the hull takes its power minus your roll. Level-ups repair the hull, so the puzzle is the order of your fights.

Sim: reading the echoes wins 49–52%, pinging blind 30%.

Play SONAR

High Roller

Abandoned: similar games already exist

Theme
A casino heist
Genre
Roguelite with permanent upgrades
Mechanic
The die is the character; loot is new faces

No stats: your d6's six faces are hit, block, parry, heal, chip or blank. Roll, then keep the face or pay a chip to reroll. Safes give new faces to swap in. Chips you don't spend are yours to keep for upgrades.

Sim: run 1 wins 9%, run 5 53%, run 10 77%.
